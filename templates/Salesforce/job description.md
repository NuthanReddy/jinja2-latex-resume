Lead Member of Technical Staff (LMTS) - Informatica
IDMC (Data Governance)
Role Overview
As a Lead Member of Technical Staff (LMTS) for the Informatica IDMC (Informatica
Intelligent Data Management Cloud) team — now an integral part of Salesforce — you
will play a pivotal role in engineering the next generation of autonomous Data Governance
and management solutions.
This is a high-impact, hybrid engineering role requiring a "full-stack" backend mindset.
You will lead the design, development, and quality strategy for scalable data services that
empower enterprises to discover, govern, and trust their data at scale. You will work with
a world-class team of engineers to integrate Informatica’s deep data expertise with
Salesforce’s ecosystem, leveraging Spark, modern Data Lake architectures, and
Generative AI to automate complex data stewardship tasks in delivering trusted data at
scale.
Responsibilities
● End-to-End Ownership: Drive the entire SDLC, from initial requirement
analysis and system design to automated testing, deployment, and
performance tuning.
● Technical Leadership: Provide architectural guidance and mentorship to a
scrum team. Lead code reviews, design sessions, and drive technical
roadmaps for Data Governance features.
● Scalable Development: Design and implement high-performance backend
services using Java/Python and Apache Spark to process massive datasets
within the IDMC framework.
● Data Architecture: Build and optimize modern Data Lake solutions, ensuring
seamless data movement and governance across cloud environments.
● Data Modeling: Architect complex data models and optimize queries across
distributed storage systems.
● Quality Champion: Implement robust automated testing frameworks and
CI/CD pipelines to ensure the reliability of mission-critical data governance
workflows.
● Cross-Cloud Collaboration: Work closely with Product Management and
Architects across Salesforce and Informatica to ensure seamless integration of
IDMC services.
Required Skills & Experience
● Experience: Minimum of 8 years of professional software development
experience in an enterprise environment.
● Programming language: Strong proficiency in Java (Multithreading, and JVM
tuning), or Python, and Object-Oriented designing.
● Big Data Processing: Strong hands-on experience with Apache Spark for
large-scale data processing and transformation.
● Database Knowledge: Detailed understanding of RDBMS concepts and
experience working with distributed storage systems.
● Engineering Excellence: Proven experience with Git, CI/CD pipelines and
Agile/Scrum methodologies.
● Quality Engineering: Experience in building automated test suites (JUnit,
Selenium, or similar) to ensure high-performance and secure code.
● Education: B.S., B.E. or B.Tech in Computer Science, or a related technical
field.
● Communication: Excellent cross-team communication and problem solving
skills
Preferred Skills (Good to Have)
● Modern Data Lakehouse: Experience with Data Lake architectures,
Lakehouse patterns, and open table formats, specifically Apache Iceberg.
● Advanced Data Stores: Experience with Graph Databases (e.g., Neo4j) and
Elasticsearch for high-speed discovery.
● AI & Generative AI: Experience with LLMs (Large Language Models), prompt
engineering, and building RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) agents to
automate data classification or metadata discovery.
● Cloud Platforms: Familiarity with AWS, Azure, or GCP infrastructure and
containerization (Docker, Kubernetes).
● Data Governance Domain: Prior experience in Data Cataloging, Data Quality,
or Metadata Management.
● Salesforce Ecosystem: Knowledge of Salesforce Data Cloud or experience
integrating third-party SaaS platforms.
● Advanced Degree: M.S. in Computer Science or a relevant field.
Why Join Us?
You will be at the forefront of the Salesforce and Informatica integration, helping shape
how the world’s leading companies manage their most valuable assets: Data. This role
offers the agility of a focused product team with the massive scale and resources of
Salesforce.
